Our Mission

AHMTAR (Advanced Human Mobility Tracking and Rehabilitation) is a pioneering initiative designed to democratize access to neurological recovery tools. We empower survivors of stroke, dementia, and traumatic brain injury to monitor their mobility and track rehabilitation progress with precision—completely free of charge.

Meet the Team

AHMTAR was founded by Neil Pal within the Cognition, Language, and Plasticity (CLaP) Lab at Chapman University, under the expert guidance of Lab Director Zed Sehyr. Together with Research Aide Vansh Bhavsar, the team is dedicated to bridging the gap between expensive clinical technology and accessible, at-home care.
